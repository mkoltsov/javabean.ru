<p>This book is a perfect guide for building castles in the sky. Most of what you learn from it is that data is hard to deal with and it's better to make fantasies about a perfect world where everyone is sharing and collaborating and both your time and budget are unlimited. </p>

<p>This must have been just one long blogpost explainining what're the differences between datawarehouses, data lakes, delta lakehouses, data fabrics and data mesh. This is key and unfortunately, I couldn't find one single source of truth that explains their differences in detail.</p>

<p>This book can only be recommended if you have plenty of time and leasurely want to learn about data architectures based on a highlevel view of someone who doesn't even talk about their practical implementation.</p>

<p>Score 2/5</p>
