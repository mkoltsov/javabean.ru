<p>One of the best books on observability, it's second edition delivers the AI update that feels so neccessary since the first book came out</p>

<p>The book has 2 parts than need to be considered in separation. </p>
<p>First, practical with lots of insights from the seasoned professionals in the field sharing their stories and best practices. I loved it, one of the best books I've read, it's  kindled my interest to try other sides of observability I missed before. Lots of fun stories, easy to read. </p>
<p>Second, I'd title as "how to sell observability and hire people to put in the observability team". Feels forced and boring to read"</p>
<p>Overall score is going to be 3.5 as the first part gets the highest possible score and and the second part gets only 2.</p>
<p>Score 3/5</p>
