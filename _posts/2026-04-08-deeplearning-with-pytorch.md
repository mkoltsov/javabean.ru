<p>Probably, the least boring book on building your own language model with PyTorch</p>

<p>The book keeps you engaged till the very end with fun examples and ,most unusually for a technical book, jokes. What's also unusual, all its Jupyter code examples worked for me.</p>

<p>Not sure how much did I learn from it, but can 100% recommend it as reading it was fun and pleasant.</p>

<p>Score 5/5</p>
